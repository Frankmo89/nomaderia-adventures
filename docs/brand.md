# Brand: the Nomaderia logo ("El Pin")

Approved by Frank on 2026-10-03. It replaces the placeholder logo (lucide `Mountain` icon + "NOMADERIA" text) and any earlier "la Ruta" files.

## The mark

- **Symbol:** a map pin. Its window shows a mountain range (your next national park) and an orange dot (the campfire at the end of the day).
- **Wordmark:** `nomader` + `IA`, set in Oswald Medium (500) and outlined in every SVG, so no font is needed to show it. `IA` is always fogata `#D97706`.
- **The name:** Nomaderia = nómada + IA, and it echoes "camaradería".
- **In running text** write "Nomaderia": capital N, no accent, the same as the domain. Never "Nomadería", "NOMADERIA" or "nomaderIA" inside a sentence.

## Rules

- **Size:** the horizontal logo is at least 120 px wide (28–36 px tall in a navbar). The symbol is at least 24 px tall; below that, use the favicon.
- **Clear space:** keep empty space on every side equal to the height of the "n".
- **Orange:** the logo is the one exception to "orange only in the Hero". `IA` and the sun dot stay fogata wherever the logo appears. Never recolor them to fix the rule.
- **Never:** stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, add shadows or gradients, redraw the pin, or retype the wordmark as live text. On a busy photo, put a forest overlay under the logo first.
- **Never** place the logo next to the NPS arrowhead. Nomaderia is not part of the National Park Service and never suggests it is.

## Slogan

- **Slogan:** "Aquí nadie se pierde… nomás se encuentra." (with the ellipsis; always "nomás" with the accent). Use it in bios, the Facebook cover, merch, link previews and as a closing line in captions.
- **What we do, in one line:** "Tú eliges el parque. Nosotros armamos el plan."
- Don't use "tu primer parque nacional" or "en español" in taglines or headlines. "Primer" shuts out people who have already been to a park, and the Spanish is obvious from the copy itself.
- Don't use "Planeado con IA, revisado por un agente certificado" as a tagline. The certified agent can appear as a plain fact (for example, a step in "how it works"), never as the slogan.

## Social sizes (for content, not the site)

- Profile picture 1080 × 1080, safe for the circle crop.
- Carousels and feed posts 1080 × 1350 (4 : 5).
- Reels and stories 1080 × 1920. Keep text out of the top 250 px, the bottom 350 px and the right 150 px.
- Every reel ends on the vertical logo and one call to action: "Haz el quiz gratis en nomaderia.com".
